About

About Ratul Hasan: background, education, experience and what they work on.

I’m a Computer Science student at Daffodil International University, and most of what I have learned so far I have learned by building things and by competing. C, C++ and Java are my working languages, and I care more about understanding an algorithm properly than about the number of frameworks I have tried.

My results have moved in one direction so far. Semester CGPA went from 3.50 in my first semester to 3.89 in my fourth, and each semester has been better than the one before it — first programming fundamentals, then problem solving, then data structures, then algorithms.

I keep showing up at programming contests. “Unlock the Algorithm”, an algorithm contest organised by DIU, and “Take Off”, the university’s problem-solving event, are two I have taken part in, and I am a regular at the hackathons that come through the department. Every one of them has taught me more about reading a problem carefully than any textbook chapter did.

Outside of it I ride motorcycles, and I am a firm believer in the way Cristiano Ronaldo works — the same effort on an ordinary Tuesday as on the day of a final. I want to go into software engineering after graduation, on a team where the code is read as carefully as it is written.
